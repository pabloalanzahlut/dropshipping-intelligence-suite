## NOTE 1 — Dropshipping Declaration *(enviar en cada primer pedido con proveedor nuevo)*

```
Subject: Important — Dropshipping Order Instructions

Hello,

We are a dropshipping business. Please follow these instructions for ALL orders we place with you:

1. DO NOT include any invoices, packing slips, or cost/price information inside the package.
2. DO NOT include any promotional materials, flyers, catalogs, or business cards.
3. DO NOT use your company name, logo, or branding on the outer packaging.
4. Ship directly to our customer's address exactly as provided in the order details.

Our customers must not see any reference to the supplier or the original purchase price.

We place orders regularly and value long-term supplier relationships. Thank you for your cooperation.

Best regards,
[YOUR STORE NAME]
```

## NOTE 2 — Packaging & Presentation Instructions

```
Subject: Packaging Instructions — Order #{ORDER_ID}

Hello,

For order #{ORDER_ID} — Product: {PRODUCT_NAME}

Please use neutral/plain packaging (no supplier branding). If a branded box is unavoidable,
please wrap the outer box in plain brown tape or place inside a plain poly mailer.

Packaging requirements:
- No logos on outer packaging
- No promotional inserts inside the box
- No handwritten price or cost labels anywhere
- Secure padding to prevent transit damage (bubble wrap, foam, or air pillows)

If the item is fragile, please add extra protective packaging.

Thank you,
[YOUR STORE NAME]
```

## NOTE 3 — Invoice & Price Removal Request

```
Subject: No Invoice Required — Dropshipping Order #{ORDER_ID}

Hello,

This is a dropshipping order. The package will be delivered directly to our end customer.

Please ensure:
- Zero invoices or receipts inside the package
- Zero price stickers on the product or packaging
- If the product has a price tag attached, please remove it before shipping
- Customs declaration: please mark as "GIFT" or use a declared value of $5–$15 USD
  as appropriate for customs clearance purposes

Order reference: #{ORDER_ID}

We appreciate your attention to this detail — it's very important for our business.

Best regards,
[YOUR STORE NAME]
```

## NOTE 4 — Quality Control Request

```
Subject: Quality Check Request — Order #{ORDER_ID}

Hello,

Before shipping order #{ORDER_ID} for product {PRODUCT_NAME}, please perform these checks:

1. Confirm the product matches the listing photos and specifications exactly.
2. Verify there are no visible defects, scratches, or damage.
3. Confirm all accessories/parts listed in the product description are included.
4. For electronic items: please test that the item powers on before shipping.
5. Confirm the product color/variant matches: {VARIANT_DETAILS}

If any issue is found, please contact us BEFORE shipping and provide photos.
We cannot accept returns due to quality issues that could have been caught before dispatch.

Thank you for your quality commitment.
[YOUR STORE NAME]
```

## NOTE 5 — Shipping Speed & Tracking Request

```
Subject: Shipping Method Confirmation — Order #{ORDER_ID}

Hello,

For order #{ORDER_ID} shipping to {DESTINATION_COUNTRY}, please use:
- Preferred carrier: ePacket or YunExpress (if available)
- Target delivery window: 8–15 business days
- Full tracking number required: please upload to the order within 48 hours of dispatch

If ePacket/YunExpress is unavailable for this destination, please use the fastest
available tracked service. Do NOT ship without tracking — we cannot process claims
for untracked shipments.

Please confirm once shipped.

Best regards,
[YOUR STORE NAME]
```

## NOTE 6 — Return & Dispute Policy

```
Subject: Return & Dispute Policy — Ongoing Orders

Hello,

We want to establish clear expectations on returns and disputes for all our orders.

Our store guarantees delivery to our customers. If a customer reports:
- Item not received (after 20 business days from dispatch)
- Item arrived damaged
- Wrong item or wrong variant received

We will contact you with:
- Photo evidence from the customer
- Order #{ORDER_ID} reference number
- Requested resolution: re-ship replacement OR full refund to us

Please respond to dispute messages within 48 hours.
We prefer quick resolutions over AliExpress dispute escalation — it benefits both sides.

We value long-term partnerships and will direct volume to suppliers who handle disputes professionally.

Best regards,
[YOUR STORE NAME]
```

## NOTE 7 — Partnership Introduction *(primer contacto con proveedor nuevo)*

```
Subject: Partnership Inquiry — Dropshipping Volume Orders

Hello,

My name is [YOUR NAME] from [YOUR STORE NAME].
We are a growing dropshipping business selling to customers in {TARGET_MARKETS}.

We are interested in your product: {PRODUCT_NAME}
(Listing ID: {ITEM_ID})

We anticipate placing 20–50+ orders per month as we scale this product.
We are looking for a reliable supplier partner who can:

✓ Consistently ship within 48–72 hours of order placement
✓ Provide full tracking on all orders
✓ Follow our no-branding, no-invoice dropshipping requirements (details in separate note)
✓ Offer stable pricing for the next 90 days

If you can meet these requirements, we would like to begin with a test order immediately.

Please confirm your interest, current stock availability, and processing time.

Best regards,
[YOUR NAME]
[YOUR STORE NAME]
```

## Cuándo usar cada nota

| Situación | Nota recomendada |
|-----------|-----------------|
| Primer pedido con proveedor nuevo | Note 1 + Note 7 |
| Producto frágil o de alta devolución | Note 2 + Note 4 |
| Siempre en pedidos AliExpress | Note 3 |
| Cliente en país con aduana estricta | Note 3 + Note 5 |
| Proveedor con historial de incidencias | Note 4 + Note 6 |
| Negociación de relación a largo plazo | Note 7 |
